# First Steps — Unit 3: My workday

A2 · **Can-do:** describe my routine and schedule · **Language:** frequency adverbs, telling the time ·
**Vocabulary:** daily tasks, office routines · **Simulation:** compare schedules to find a free slot
Company: Pampa Seguros (fictional insurance company, office in Porto Alegre; office work only — claims, customer calls, admin)
Cast: Camila (claims assistant), Diego (claims analyst), Renata (team leader), Felipe (IT support)
Story: 3A Camila's "A day in my life" post on the company blog · 3B Diego's routine + "How often do you…?" · 3C Camila and Diego compare their calendars to find a free slot for a meeting with Renata
Common-mistake thread: adverb position ("I go always…" → "I always go…"), "at the morning" (→ in the morning), "It's 8 and 30" (→ eight thirty / half past eight)

New in each lesson: **3A** I + always / usually / often / sometimes / never, times (at 8:30, in the morning) · **3B** How often…? / What time does…? + once a week / every day, adverbs with be ("She is always busy") · **3C** free / busy, Are you free at…? / I'm busy at…, How about…? + a short message proposing a time

Plan from 2026-11-19: **one Reading Text + one Presentation per lesson** (something new every lesson); practice activities can be added later with "+ Add".
