# First Steps — Unit 2: My team and my role

A2 · **Can-do:** say who does what in my team and what I do · **Language:** present simple (he / she works…),
there is / there are · **Vocabulary:** departments, tasks, responsibilities · **Simulation:** explain your team to a new colleague
Company: Costa Verde Energy (fictional energy supplier, office in Recife; office work only — billing, customer service, HR)
Cast: Marta (customer service manager), Lucas (new customer service assistant), Rafael (billing analyst), Júlia (HR assistant)
Story: Lucas is new — 2A Marta's welcome email (who does what) · 2B Lucas asks Rafael about the team · 2C a month later Lucas explains the team to a newer colleague
Brazilian-mistake thread: "He work in Billing" (missing -s) · "She don't…"

New in each lesson: **2A** he / she + -s for tasks · **2B** Does…? / doesn't / What does she do? · **2C** there is / there are, How many…?, "If you have a question about…, ask…" + a short message about your week
